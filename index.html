Podcast Math

You are not behind on podcasts. You are behind on the arithmetic.

Boundary: counting arithmetic only Podcast Math multiplies and divides the numbers you type - hours, speeds, weeks. It never tells you what to listen to, how fast to live, or what your time is worth.

The drain truth

Listening hours times speed is the only honest exchange rate. Seven listening hours a week at 1.5x covers 10.5 content hours - the 40-hour backlog was never a personality flaw, it is a 3.8-week project.

The queue truth

If shows publish 12 hours a week and you cover 10.5, the queue grows by 1.5 hours a week forever. The fix is fewer subscriptions, not more guilt.

The episode truth

The 47-minute episode is a 31.3-minute episode at 1.5x, and the commute is 22 minutes. Nobody ever finished it on the drive in; they finished the arithmetic instead.

Run your numbers
Podcast Math is exact arithmetic over your own hours - backlog, queue and episodes, nothing more.
